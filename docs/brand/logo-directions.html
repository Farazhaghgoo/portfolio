Faraz Haghgoo / Identity exploration / 01

A mark that behaves
like you build.

Three vector sketches for Cobalt Studio. Designed in one colour first. These are directions, not final logos.

01 / Interlock

Personal + engineered

Your initials share a crossbar. Two letters, one connected structure. The strongest link between the mark and your name.

Motion idea: the middle bar slides into place, connecting F to H.

Faraz Haghgoo

02 / Offset

Modular + playful

Three solid pieces make an F. Simple enough for a favicon, with a modular shape language that can spread through the site.

Motion idea: the pieces separate, rearrange, then snap back to F.

Faraz Haghgoo

03 / Soft Circuit

Human + expressive

A custom lowercase fh with rounded, continuous-feeling strokes. More approachable, with the character of a personal signature.

Motion idea: the strokes draw on, then gently flex on interaction.

Faraz Haghgoo
Recommendation: 01 / Interlock. A personal mark with structural logic; borrow the motion of 02.Next: refine geometry, spacing, small-size clarity and one purposeful animation.
